Arrangement of "Owl" from Link's Awakening

This is a cover of the song "フクロウ" (Owl) from the 2019 remake of Link's Awakening for the Nintendo Switch. This cover was made using an emulated Game Boy soundchip via Thaumoc, as a reference to the original Game Boy version of Link's Awakening.

"Tal Tal Heights - Link's Awakening" and "SWORD - DELTARUNE" Mashup

A mashup of Tal Tal Heights from The Legend of Zelda - Link's Awakening for the Game Boy, and SWORD from DELTARUNE: Chapter 3.

SWORD was already composed to sound like a Zelda overworld song, and I think it may bear a particular resemblance to Tal Tal Heights, so I wanted to make a mashup that highlights the similarities between these two songs.

This mashup was made by using gbs2midi and Thaumoc to edit the music of Link's Awakening. I also used this sheet music of SWORD transcribed by gubgub.

Tal Tal Heights and SWORD mashup midi download.

Unfinished Famicom N163 Cover of Katamaritaino

Back in April 2025, I had fun making a cover of the intro of Katamaritaino where the acoustic guitar is replicated by 6 channels of the N163 chip, one channel for each string of a guitar. I lost interest in the idea of covering the entire song, but I'm still proud of the little bit that I made. WIP Katamaritaino cover FamiStudio project download.
